## Project-specific

### Which schema.org types are included?
LocalBusiness plus common subtypes (Plumber, HVACBusiness, Electrician, Dentist, Restaurant, and more). Edit the dropdown in `index.html` to add others.

### Where do I paste the output?
Inside the `<head>` of the page it describes, usually the home page or contact page.

### How do I check it is valid?
Use Google's Rich Results Test and the Schema Markup Validator after publishing.

### Will schema improve my rankings?
It helps search engines understand your business; it is not a ranking guarantee.

### Why are my hours rejected?
Each row needs at least one day and different open/close times. Times can be `09:00`, `9am`, or `5:30pm`.
